About the Project

OAn exploratory data analysis (EDA) was performed to understand the video game market using data visualization techniques. Sales metrics were analyzed regionally to discover the profile of best-selling games, and finally, hypotheses were tested to identify certain user behaviors.

The project consists of an analysis of video game sales, taking into account factors such as platform, genre, reviews, regions, etc. The objective is to understand the video game market and how games become commercial successes.
